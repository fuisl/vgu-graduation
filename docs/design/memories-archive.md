# After the ceremony: curated archive, timeline and guest prints

The "After" leg of the experience arc (`product/vision.md`, epic #20): the curated public archive at `/memories` (#75) and the cohort timeline at `/memories/timeline` (#76). Also the guest "Request a print" action in the event gallery (#74), which is experimental. All three are brand pages and reuse the shipped blue-and-white language; the layout decisions marked **for owner review** below are new and are surfaced for review (`AGENTS.md`, design authority).

## Routing (decided 2026-10-19)

- **`/gallery` stays as it is.** It is the event gallery, open to everyone since 2026-09-30, and lists every visible photo and wish (`GET /gallery`, `GET /wishes`). Nothing about the ceremony-day flow changes.
- **`/gallery` carousel and dialog (#78).** The carousel autoplays every 3s only without reduced motion and only in a visible tab. A "Pause slideshow" / "Play slideshow" secondary `Cta` (44px) sits under the stage beside the position counter; any touch, click, card focus or arrow on the stage stops autoplay for good (Play resumes it), and hovering pauses it only while the pointer is there. The counter is `aria-live="off"` while autoplay moves it and polite once the guest does. Faded side cards keep their photo but do not draw their text (it could not reach AA at that opacity) and are `aria-hidden`. "View details" opens a modal `<dialog>` (`showModal()`, so the page behind is inert): the Close button takes focus, Tab and Shift+Tab cycle inside it, Escape or a tap on the scrim closes it, body scroll is locked, and focus returns to the card.
- **`/memories` is the curated archive** and **`/memories/timeline`** the timeline. They read only `GET /gallery/public` and `GET /wishes/public`, which return items that are visible and flagged public by a moderator, so an unflagged photo or wish can never appear there (tested in `apps/web/app/(memories)/memories/archive.test.tsx`). The timeline also reads `GET /event` for its chapter boundaries.
- Both pages render per request in the guest's language (#9, ADR-010); their API data is served from the Next fetch cache (revalidated every 30 to 60 seconds). Their fetches carry the contract's `CACHE_TAGS`: `gallery-public`, `wishes-public` (and `event` on the timeline), the tags admin moderation and public-flag writes return in `revalidateTags`, so a takedown or a curation change shows as soon as the admin action revalidates them.
- Images are derivatives only (`/media/{publicId}/thumb|display`): the `thumb` with a `srcset` up to `display`, with true pixel widths from the photo's dimensions, and the photo links to its `display` derivative. Originals never leave `grad-originals`.
- **Links.** The header keeps its four links. `/memories` is linked from the footer ("Memories", after the four), the landing gallery band ("Curated archive and timeline") and the `/gallery` hero. The timeline is linked from `/memories` (hero primary action and the closing band) and links back.

## `/memories` (curated archive)

- **Bands.** Blue hero (eyebrow "Archive / Curated", headline, one lead line, primary "Walk the timeline", secondary "Event gallery"); white compact "Photos" band; blue "Wishes" band; white compact closing band pointing to the timeline, event gallery and guestbook.
- **Photos.** Square thumbs in a grid, two columns on phones and four on desktop, like the landing teaser. The first four load eagerly, the rest lazily. **For owner review:** a small mono count next to the heading.
- **Wishes on blue. For owner review:** wishes sit on a blue band as large type separated by hairlines (`--brand-line`), with the author as a mono label, two columns on desktop. No cards on blue. Each wish uses `content-visibility: auto` so a long list stays cheap.
- **States.** Nothing curated yet (both listings empty): one white band explaining that the organizers curate after the ceremony, with links to the event gallery and guestbook, and the hero's primary action becomes "Open the event gallery". A failed listing degrades only its band ("temporarily unavailable" with a retry link). More than 500 photos (five pages) shows a note that only the most recent are listed.

## `/memories/timeline` (cohort timeline)

- **Chapters.** Entries are ordered oldest first. With the event configuration the ceremony day splits into **Arrival** (before `startsAt`), **Ceremony** (`startsAt` to `endsAt`, or the calendar feed's two-hour default) and **Celebration** (the rest of that day), with **Before the day** and **After the day** around it, all in the event's time zone. If `GET /event` fails, chapters fall back to days, or to hours when everything is on one day. Empty chapters are skipped. The logic is the pure `apps/web/lib/memories/timeline.ts`.
- **Layout. For owner review:** a vertical rail (one hairline) with a small square blue marker (7px, 2px radius) per entry and the time in blue mono above the content. Photos taken within 30 minutes of each other (up to six, with no wish between) form one entry shown as a square grid, two columns on phones and three on desktop, so a burst reads as one moment and phones load thumbs; a lone photo keeps its own aspect ratio. Wishes use the landing's soft-blue quote panel. Each chapter has a mono range eyebrow ("09:00–11:00"), an h3 title and a photo and wish count.
- **Chapter index. For owner review:** a list of chapter links (44px rows, title and range) above the timeline on phones, and a sticky column beside it on desktop (one breakpoint, 960px). No header change and no pill navigation.
- **Performance.** Each chapter is `content-visibility: auto` with an intrinsic size; only the first photo entry loads eagerly; `srcset` and `sizes` let phones pick the 640px thumb.
- **States.** Empty (nothing curated): an explanation and links to the event gallery and guestbook, no index. Both listings failed: an error line with a retry link. One failed: a notice above the timeline. Loading: `memories/loading.tsx`, a blue band with "Loading memories…".
- **Motion.** None.

## Guest print request (`/gallery`, #74, experimental)

- **Flag.** Off unless the web app's server-side env `PRINT_GUEST_REQUESTS_ENABLED=true`, the same name and meaning as the API flag; set both. The web flag only decides whether the action is shown and is read at request time (no rebuild). The API enforces its own flag (403 when off).
- **Where.** In the gallery's photo dialog, below the photo, for photos only. `GET /api/print` tells the gallery whether to show it (`{enabled, signedIn, perHour}`, `private, no-store`); any failure hides the action, so printing never affects the gallery. Signed-out visitors see a line asking them to open their invitation first.
- **Flow.** "Request a print" (secondary) → a confirmation ("Print one copy of this photo at the venue? Each invitation can request 3 prints an hour.") with "Confirm print" (primary) and "Cancel" → "Sending…" → a success line, or an error line. Focus moves to the step's main control or message.
- **BFF.** `POST /api/print {photoPublicId}` reads the HttpOnly `inv` cookie server-side and calls `POST /print/jobs {photoPublicId, copies: 1}` with it as a bearer credential, uncached; it requires a JSON body (no cross-site form posts). Statuses pass through: 401/404/410 ask the guest to reopen their invitation link, 403 "Printing isn't available right now", 409 "This photo can't be printed…", 429 names the limit and the wait from `Retry-After` ("Try again in about 40 minutes"), network and 5xx offer "Try again".
