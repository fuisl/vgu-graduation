# The Digital Locket, the curated archive, the timeline and guest prints

The guest camera and live feed at `/gallery` (the **Digital Locket**) and the wishes page `/wishes` are the "During" leg of the experience arc (`product/vision.md`); the curated public archive at `/memories` (#75) and the cohort timeline at `/memories/timeline` (#76) are the "After" leg (epic #20). Also the guest "Request a print" action in the locket's photo dialog (#74), which is experimental. All are brand pages and reuse the shipped blue-and-white language; the layout decisions marked **for owner review** below are new and are surfaced for review (`AGENTS.md`, design authority).

## Camera-first redesign (2026-10-19)

The owner's brief: open the camera right away, cut the text, stop repeating information, and make each screen's one action obvious at a glance. Event facts, language and text size live in the site header (the menubar); these pages do not repeat them. Removed from `/gallery`, `/wishes` and `/polaroid`: the blue heroes with eyebrows and lead lines, the explanatory paragraphs, the "Take a disposable photo" / "Archive" / "Back to the gallery" / "Photo instead" buttons, the gallery's filter, card size and grid/carousel toggles, the carousel and its autoplay, and the "From / To" card metadata.

## `/gallery`: the Digital Locket

- **Order.** A blue compact band with the camera (the page's `h1`, "Digital Locket", is visually hidden), then a white compact band, "Everyone's photos". Nothing else. **For owner review:** the camera band has no top padding, so the viewfinder sits right under the header.
- **Viewfinder. For owner review:** a square (`aspect-ratio: 1`), because the capture is a centre-cropped square: what the guest sees is what is sent. Dark photo surface (`--brand-ink`), full-bleed with square corners on phones (at 960px and below), 4px radius and at most 36rem wide on desktop. Its side is capped by the viewport height minus the header and the controls, so the shutter is never below the fold (checked at 375x740).
- **Controls. For owner review:** one row under the viewfinder: shots left as a mono label on the left (only once the API has said how many), a 76px white ring shutter in the middle (`aria-label` "Take photo"), and a "Flip" text button on the right, shown only when the device has a second camera (`aria-label` "Flip to the rear camera" / "...selfie camera", which starts with the visible word). The rear camera is the default (guests photograph the stage); the selfie preview and capture are mirrored. Under the row, one muted line: "Photos are public: shown in the hall and kept in the graduation archive."
- **Take, then send.** The shutter freezes the shot in the viewfinder and the row becomes "Retake" (secondary) and "Send" (primary, `PaperPlanePixel`); focus moves to Send, and Escape retakes. Send hands the shot to the outbox and the camera is ready again at once, focus back on the shutter. A retake costs nothing: only a sent shot spends one of the roll's 36.
- **Camera states**, each in plain words on the viewfinder with one clear action:
  - checking the invitation (`/api/me`, QA P1-2): an empty viewfinder and a disabled shutter, so a signed-out visitor never sees the permission prompt first;
  - signed out: "Open your invitation first", one line, and "How to open your invitation" (primary, to `/me`);
  - starting (getUserMedia pending, i.e. the browser's prompt is up): "Allow camera access when your browser asks.";
  - blocked (`NotAllowedError`, `SecurityError`): "Camera access is off", how to turn it on (site settings from the address bar, allow Camera, reload), then "Use phone camera" (primary, a file input with `capture="environment"`, which opens the phone's own camera app and needs no browser permission) and "Choose from library" (secondary, the same input without `capture`);
  - no camera or no secure context (`NotFoundError`, `OverconstrainedError`, no `mediaDevices`): "No camera here" with the same two actions;
  - busy or failed to start (`NotReadableError`, anything else): "The camera didn't start", "Close that app, then try again", "Try again" (primary, restarts the stream) and "Choose from library";
  - roll used up (the API's `shotsRemaining` is 0, or a 409): "Your roll is used up", no action.
  A photo from the phone camera or library is square-cropped and re-encoded as a JPEG (at most 2048px) in the browser, which also drops its metadata (location, device); if the browser cannot decode it, the file goes as it is and the API decides (415 asks for another). It then goes through the same Retake / Send step. The pure error mapping is `cameraProblem` in `gallery/locket-logic.ts` (tested).
- **Privacy and battery.** The stream stops when the tab is hidden and when the page unmounts (the camera light goes off) and starts again when the tab is visible. No audio is requested.
- **Outbox** (`gallery/use-shot-queue.ts`). A sent shot shows at once at the top of the feed with a mono status tag: "Sending"; "Offline" when the phone has no network (it sends by itself on the `online` event, and "Retry" sends it now); "Not sent" with "Retry" when trying again can work (network, 503, a bad answer), or with "Remove" when it cannot (signed out, roll full, too large, unsupported); then "Sent". One status line under the camera says what happened in plain words (`role="status"`), using the `camera.error.*` messages from `lib/memories/camera-upload.ts`. Once sent, the shot is remembered as an own post (`lib/memories/own-posts.ts`) and the live feed carries it as "developing". Captures live in memory only; their object URLs are revoked on removal and unmount.
- **Feed** (`GalleryView kind="photos"`, data from `useLiveGallery`, ADR-014, see `live-surfaces.md`). **For owner review:** square tiles, newest first, three columns on phones (4px gaps, 2px radius) and five on desktop; the 640px thumb with a `srcset` up to the display derivative; the first six load eagerly. A tile opens the photo dialog. States: "Loading photos…", "No photos yet. Take the first one.", "Photos can't load right now. Try again." and the partial notice.
- **Developing. For owner review:** the guest's own photo, from its local preview, in greyscale with a mono "Developing" tag until the processed photo replaces it in colour. Not openable (there is nothing to print yet). No animation.
- **New arrivals. For owner review:** they go straight in while the top of the feed is in view; while the guest reads further down, they are held behind a sticky "N new photos" button (solid blue, 44px, 4px radius) in a zero-height holder, so neither the button nor the arrivals move what the guest is looking at. The button shows them, scrolls to the feed heading and focuses it; scrolling back up shows them too. Own posts are never held; an older item arriving in a catch-up is never counted as new. The rule is `arrivals` in `gallery/locket-logic.ts` (tested).
- **Connection.** Only when the live feed is `offline`: one muted line, "You're offline. New posts appear when you're back online." Nothing while it works, including while polling.
- **Photo dialog (QA P1-1).** A modal `<dialog>` (`showModal()`, so the page behind is inert): a bar with the time (mono, blue) and "Close", which takes focus; the display derivative; the print request. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle inside it, Escape or a tap on the scrim closes it, body scroll is locked, and focus returns to the tile.
- **`/polaroid`** redirects (307) to `/gallery`, so shared links and the guest profile's link keep working.

## `/wishes`

- **Order. For owner review:** a blue compact band with the composer, then a white compact band, "Everyone's wishes". The composer sits on blue like the camera (action on blue, feed on white): the `h1` "Leave a wish for the class" names the message field (`aria-labelledby`); the fields are white boxes with ink text and the accent focus ring (on blue only); one "Send" (`PaperPlanePixel`) beside an optional "Your name" field (blank uses the name on the invitation; its full label is visually hidden); one muted line, "Wishes are public: shown in the hall and kept in the graduation archive."
- **Signed out** (`/api/me`, like the camera): the heading becomes "Open your invitation first", with one line and "How to open your invitation", instead of a form that would fail on send.
- **Optimistic posting** (`wishes/use-wish-queue.ts`). Send clears the field, keeps focus in it, and puts the wish on top of the feed: "Sending", "Waiting for connection" (sends by itself when back online; "Retry", "Edit"), "Not sent" with "Retry" and "Edit" (429, 5xx, network) or "Edit" only (signed out, invalid, expired, length), then "Sent". "Edit" puts the text and name back in the composer. A status line under the composer says what happened. The saved wish is remembered as an own post so the live feed shows it at once.
- **Feed** (`GalleryView kind="wishes"`): type and hairlines like the archive's wishes, the author and time as mono labels, with the same live behaviour, "N new wishes" button and offline line as the photos.

## Routing and links

- **`/memories` is the curated archive** and **`/memories/timeline`** the timeline. They read only `GET /gallery/public` and `GET /wishes/public`, which return items that are visible and flagged public by a moderator, so an unflagged photo or wish can never appear there (tested in `apps/web/app/(memories)/memories/archive.test.tsx`). The timeline also reads `GET /event` for its chapter boundaries.
- Both pages render per request in the guest's language (#9, ADR-010); their API data is served from the Next fetch cache (revalidated every 30 to 60 seconds). Their fetches carry the contract's `CACHE_TAGS`: `gallery-public`, `wishes-public` (and `event` on the timeline), the tags admin moderation and public-flag writes return in `revalidateTags`, so a takedown or a curation change shows as soon as the admin action revalidates them.
- Images are derivatives only (`/media/{publicId}/thumb|display`): the `thumb` with a `srcset` up to `display`, with true pixel widths from the photo's dimensions, and the photo links to its `display` derivative. Originals never leave `grad-originals`.
- **Links.** `/gallery` ("Camera") and `/wishes` are in the menubar; `/memories` is not. It is linked from the footer ("Memories", the footer's first link); the locket, wishes and landing pages do not repeat it. The timeline is linked from the `/memories` hero and links back.

## `/memories` (curated archive)

- **Bands.** Blue hero (headline and one primary action, "Walk the timeline"); white compact "From the day" band (photos); blue "Words for the class" band (wishes). No eyebrows, lead line or closing link band.
- **Photos.** Square thumbs in a grid, two columns on phones and four on desktop, like the landing teaser. The first four load eagerly, the rest lazily. **For owner review:** a small mono count next to the heading.
- **Wishes on blue. For owner review:** wishes sit on a blue band as large type separated by hairlines (`--brand-line`), with the author as a mono label, two columns on desktop. No cards on blue. Each wish uses `content-visibility: auto` so a long list stays cheap.
- **States.** Nothing curated yet (both listings empty): one white band, "Nothing curated yet" and one line saying the organizers choose after the ceremony; the hero's action becomes "See everyone's photos" (to `/gallery`). A failed listing degrades only its band ("temporarily unavailable" with a retry link). More than 500 photos (five pages) shows a note that only the most recent are listed.

## `/memories/timeline` (cohort timeline)

- **Chapters.** Entries are ordered oldest first. With the event configuration the ceremony day splits into **Arrival** (before `startsAt`), **Ceremony** (`startsAt` to `endsAt`, or the calendar feed's two-hour default) and **Celebration** (the rest of that day), with **Before the day** and **After the day** around it, all in the event's time zone. If `GET /event` fails, chapters fall back to days, or to hours when everything is on one day. Empty chapters are skipped. The logic is the pure `apps/web/lib/memories/timeline.ts`.
- **Layout. For owner review:** a vertical rail (one hairline) with a small square blue marker (7px, 2px radius) per entry and the time in blue mono above the content. Photos taken within 30 minutes of each other (up to six, with no wish between) form one entry shown as a square grid, two columns on phones and three on desktop, so a burst reads as one moment and phones load thumbs; a lone photo keeps its own aspect ratio. Wishes use the landing's soft-blue quote panel. The chapters band has its count as the `h2`, with no eyebrow; each chapter has a mono range eyebrow ("09:00–11:00"), an h3 title and a photo and wish count.
- **Chapter index. For owner review:** a list of chapter links (44px rows, title and range) above the timeline on phones, and a sticky column beside it on desktop (one breakpoint, 960px). No header change and no pill navigation.
- **Performance.** Each chapter is `content-visibility: auto` with an intrinsic size; only the first photo entry loads eagerly; `srcset` and `sizes` let phones pick the 640px thumb.
- **Hero.** The headline only: no eyebrow, date line, lead or back link (the footer links `/memories`).
- **States.** Empty (nothing curated): one line and "See everyone's photos" (secondary, to `/gallery`), no index. Both listings failed: an error line with a retry link. One failed: a notice above the timeline. Loading: `memories/loading.tsx`, a blue band with "Loading memories…".
- **Motion.** None.

## Guest print request (`/gallery` photo dialog, #74, experimental)

- **Flag.** Off unless the web app's server-side env `PRINT_GUEST_REQUESTS_ENABLED=true`, the same name and meaning as the API flag; set both. The web flag only decides whether the action is shown and is read at request time (no rebuild). The API enforces its own flag (403 when off).
- **Where.** In the locket's photo dialog, below the photo. Secondary: it is never on the camera or the feed itself. `GET /api/print` tells the gallery whether to show it (`{enabled, signedIn, perHour}`, `private, no-store`); any failure hides the action, so printing never affects the gallery. Signed-out visitors see a line asking them to open their invitation first.
- **Flow.** "Request a print" (secondary) → a confirmation ("Print one copy of this photo at the venue? Each invitation can request 3 prints an hour.") with "Confirm print" (primary) and "Cancel" → "Sending…" → a success line, or an error line. Focus moves to the step's main control or message.
- **BFF.** `POST /api/print {photoPublicId}` reads the HttpOnly `inv` cookie server-side and calls `POST /print/jobs {photoPublicId, copies: 1}` with it as a bearer credential, uncached; it requires a JSON body (no cross-site form posts). Statuses pass through: 401/404/410 ask the guest to reopen their invitation link, 403 "Printing isn't available right now", 409 "This photo can't be printed…", 429 names the limit and the wait from `Retry-After` ("Try again in about 40 minutes"), network and 5xx offer "Try again".
