# Moodboard

Store external references under `docs/design/references/` and approved screenshots under `docs/design/reference/`.
For every reference record what we borrow, what we do not borrow, and which GRAD surface it informs.

## Cantor8 (primary reference)
[cantor8.io](https://www.cantor8.io) informed the October 2026 redesign ([redesign-2026-10.md](redesign-2026-10.md)). It is a reference for proportion, not a template.

**Borrowed**
- Proportion: a saturated royal blue and white in roughly equal measure, as full-bleed bands that alternate. Blue is a surface.
- A minimal navigation: logo left, one segmented pill of links, one call to action on the right; on mobile a Menu button that opens a full-screen blue sheet.
- Quiet grotesk display type with tight tracking, sentence case, and small uppercase mono labels (often ending in ` ...`).
- A call to action with a square tile holding a pixel-style arrow.
- One signature visual instead of many decorative ones (for us, the ASCII sculpture).

**Not borrowed**
- Its assets, copy, logo, exact layout or section order.
- Its product storytelling. GRAD leads with event facts.
- Anything that needs more than one motif (no animated backgrounds, glows or decorative gradients).

**Informs:** every brand surface, through `Section`, `Cta`, `PillGroup` and `BrandEyebrow` (`components.md`).

## Vercel Ship
Borrow whitespace, composition discipline and selective interactive spectacle (the `/guest/prototype` badge). Do not copy proprietary assets or exact layouts.

## Firecrawl
Borrow monospace accents, ASCII/pixel language and technical texture (the sculpture, the pixel arrow, the Fuisloy pixel logo). Do not copy branding, exact illustrations or generic SaaS structure.
