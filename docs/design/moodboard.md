# Moodboard
Store external references under `docs/design/references/` and approved screenshots under `docs/design/golden/`.
For every reference record what we borrow, what we do not borrow, and which GRAD surface it informs.
## Vercel Ship
Borrow whitespace, grid discipline, monochrome composition and selective interactive spectacle. Do not copy proprietary assets or exact layouts.
## Firecrawl
Borrow monospace accents, ASCII/pixel language and technical texture. Do not copy branding, exact illustrations or generic SaaS structure.
## Golden screens
Once approved, a GRAD screenshot is a stronger reference than external inspiration. Maintain desktop and mobile goldens for critical flows.
