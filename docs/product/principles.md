# Product Principles
1. **Invitation first.**
2. **One experience, not many apps.** The invitation/pass connects event utilities.
3. **90% restraint, 10% spectacle.**
4. **Physical ↔ digital.** Phones, displays, camera and prints reinforce one event.
5. **Local ownership.** Private/event data remains under project control.
6. **Graceful failure.** 3D, printer or translation failure must not break critical flows.
7. **Archive quality.** Preserve originals privately; serve optimized derivatives.
8. **Consent, retention and takedown.** Decided 2026-09-27. The organizing team owns and is responsible for guest data (names, RSVPs, photos, wishes); no university sign-off is required. Guests are covered by a visible notice at the point of upload, not a per-guest opt-in checkbox — the guest list is small and trusted, so photos and wishes are not reviewed before display, but an admin can hide one quickly and must remove one on request. The archive, including public gallery entries, is retained for four years to match the archive mission; deletion (including originals and offsite backups) is a manual admin action on takedown request, not an automatic expiry. No special handling for minors or non-consenting bystanders is planned — the ceremony is an adults-only graduate/family context.

**Consent notice wording** (for the guest camera and wish-submission upload flows, #62/#63): "By uploading, your photo or message may be shown on the event display and gallery, and kept in the four-year graduation archive. Contact an organizer to have something removed."
